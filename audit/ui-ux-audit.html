Movement Matters Bali: site audit

All 19 routes plus the 404 page were captured with agent-browser at desktop (1440×900) and mobile (390×844), with automated DOM checks for overflow, tap targets, font size and image sizing, then reviewed by eye. Below: every issue found, ranked by severity, with the fix. The storage optimisation that was applied is summarised at the end.

What works

  • A clear, warm brand palette and consistent hairline and numbered-list system across every page.
  • No horizontal page scroll at 390px or 1440px. Overflowing elements are all clipped inside their containers.
  • Videos are poster-first: the MP4 only loads on click, so pages stay light.
  • Every page has exactly one h1, a skip link, labelled icon buttons, a keyboard-operable lightbox and a reduced-motion fallback.

Findings

Severity: Critical breaks a task or loses trust; High clearly hurts conversion or quality; Medium is visible polish or consistency debt; Low is refinement.

Unsuitable or weak images

01High

Hero and Gyrotonic photos are low-resolution and off-message

Home hero (slides 1–5), GYROTONIC® hero, home "Four ways" card 02

The hero slider uses the 2013–2015 WordPress slider photos (1280–1423px wide) stretched to a 1360–1440px full-bleed frame, so every slide is soft. Slide 2, reused as the GYROTONIC® page hero and service card, is a blurred close-up of a cast-iron weight plate. It reads as "gym", not a calm, specialist studio, and shows no person doing the method. On slide 1 the headline sits over the busiest part of the frame, with the subject's leg running through "Pilates Studio".

Fix: Use the May 2026 shoot (already used for the team portraits) or reshoot: one person on the GYROTONIC® Pulley Tower, plus wide shots of the studio. Full-bleed sources should be at least 2400px wide. Place the headline over calm areas (sky, wall, floor), or pick frames with negative space on the left.
02High

Close-ups that don't read at card size (Rolfing®)

Home "Four ways" card 03, Rolfing® hero, Rolfing® "Body as a whole", blog "Physical Therapy" card

The Rolfing® visuals are extreme crops: a black T-shirt with a hand, and bare skin on a back. At card size the Rolfing® card is a dark blob. The hero (source 1500×614) is upscaled and soft, and the bare-skin close-up feels clinical and ambiguous for a first impression.

Fix: Show the practitioner and the client together in a wider frame (face and context visible). Keep the abstract close-up only as a secondary, smaller image.
03High

Bad crops, watermarks and phone snapshots

Anatomy in 3D, Continuing Education, Contact, Media, Hotels & Services, Pricing, Teacher Training

  • Anatomy in 3D hero: both women are cropped at the chin. Their heads are missing.
  • Continuing Education hero: foreheads cut off at the top edge.
  • Pricing hero and the Anatomy video poster: a vertical photographer credit or watermark is visible on the right edge.
  • Contact hero: a tilted phone photo of the stone sign with a parked car and road. It looks accidental, not welcoming.
  • Media hero: the magazine cover is cropped mid-masthead ("Hello Bali" and "Hot List" are cut in half).
  • Hotels & Services hero: generic foliage against a wall, which says nothing about stays or services.
  • Teacher Training "Graduate stories" poster: a top-down frame with the subjects' heads cropped.
Fix: Set per-image objectPosition (as the hero slider already does), or pick different photos. Remove watermarked frames. Use the studio entrance path photo (already on Contact) as the Contact hero, and a cropped photo of a guesthouse or café for Hotels.
04High

Reformer Rental uses WhatsApp video stills

/reformer-rental: hero and video poster

Both images are frames grabbed from a compressed WhatsApp video. They are visibly blurry, with blocking artefacts, at full width. This is the page that asks visitors to pay to rent equipment.

Fix: Use a sharp photo of an empty, ready Reformer in the open-air studio. Keep the video, but give it a real poster.
05Medium

The same photo shoot repeats everywhere

Ubud Pilates, Reformer Classes, Group Schedule, Teacher Training, Continuing Ed, Move Well, Blog

One 2010s shoot (instructor in a pink top, bald man in a white vest, woman in a purple top) appears as the Ubud Pilates hero, two of its four "Ways to train" cards and its CTA band, the Reformer Classes hero and CTA, the Teacher Training hero, the Move Well card 03 and a blog card. Ubud Pilates shows near-identical frames three times on one page. The repetition makes the site feel templated and dated next to the fresh 2026 team portraits.

Fix: Use each photo once. Map images to pages before building, and commission one more shoot (private session, group class, teacher training, rental).
06Medium

The gallery mixes Instagram squares with DSLR photos

/ubud-pilates-reformer-classes "Inside the studio"

All 19 photos are shown: 1016px square Instagram crops (2018/2023) next to 2784px DSLR frames, with clashing colour grades. The masonry ends ragged, with the third column about 150px shorter.

Fix: Curate 8–10 photos with the same grade and aspect ratio (all 4:3 or all 3:2) in a fixed grid. Move the rest to the lightbox.

Layout and alignment

07Critical

Sections can render blank (content hidden until scroll animation fires)

Site-wide: Reveal, RevealText, ImageReveal (opacity 0 until whileInView)

Every block starts at opacity: 0 and only appears once Motion's in-view observer fires. On a fast scroll, a jump-link, slow hydration on a weak phone connection, or a full-page capture, whole sections stay white. The Anatomy page captured with about 1,100px of empty space after the hero (the content is there, just invisible). The same pattern affects print and any no-JS view.

Fix: Make content visible by default and animate only as an enhancement. Add a js class on <html> and hide only under .js, or use initial={false} for anything above or near the fold. Lower the threshold (amount: 0) and add a 1.2s safety timeout that reveals everything.
08High

The floating WhatsApp button covers content

All pages, desktop and mobile

The 56px green button sits on top of the hero slide index ("Rolfing® · Structural Integration"), the instructor h1 on mobile ("Sook Fun Chen" runs under it), the "This week" day tabs on the mobile schedule and the pricing tabs. Its saturated WhatsApp green is also the loudest colour on a warm, muted site.

Fix: Hide it while the hero or footer is in view (IntersectionObserver), add bottom: max(16px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom)), and give the last section bottom padding. Consider a brown or cream button with a WhatsApp glyph to match the palette.
09Medium

Scroll-jacked "Four ways to move better" section

Home, desktop ≥1024px (HorizontalScroll)

The section pins and converts about 1,600px of vertical scroll into sideways motion. Cards enter cropped at both viewport edges (card 01 is half off-screen when 02 is centred), and there is no progress indicator or way to skip. Trackpad users often feel the page is stuck. The heading and intro also scroll away on the left, leaving a large white gap under them.

Fix: Replace it with a static 4-up grid (it fits at 1440px), or a normal carousel with arrows and a progress bar. If the pin stays, add a "01 / 04" progress counter and cut the travel distance by about half.
10Medium

Inconsistent left edges and centring

Home (mobile), Reformer Rental, instructor pages, Group Schedule

  • Home mobile: "Plan your first visit" is centred while every line around it is left-aligned.
  • Team strip (home and instructor pages, mobile): the carousel starts flush at the screen edge (0px) instead of the 20px gutter everything else uses.
  • Reformer Rental "Rates": the block is inset about 40px from the container's left line, so the "Rates" title doesn't line up with the hero title or the numbered row above.
  • Group Schedule hero: the third CTA, "Email", is a bare text link that wraps under the buttons on desktop and sits beside an outline button on mobile.
  • Page heroes: the right column (lead and CTAs) is top-aligned on Ubud Pilates and Reformer Rental, bottom-aligned on GYROTONIC® and vertically centred on Contact. Pick one rule (bottom-aligned to the h1 baseline).
Fix: Every block should start on .container-x padding. Give the team carousel scroll-padding-inline and padding-inline equal to the gutter. Make "Email" a third outline button or drop it.
11Medium

Orphans and ragged grids

Stats bands, Group Schedule, Blog, Teacher Training, Policies, Move Well

  • Stats band, mobile: 5 items in 2 columns leave "2 GYROTONIC® Towers" alone on the last row.
  • Group Schedule "Classes": 7 cards in 2 columns, so "Jumpboard" sits alone next to an empty cell.
  • Blog: the bento grid leaves 2 empty cells at the bottom right. The last row has two small cards and a void.
  • Teacher Training "Three levels": the Level 3 title wraps to 2 lines, so the three descriptions don't share a baseline.
  • Pricing "Policies": 4 columns with 1 to 5 items each; the bottom edge staircases.
  • Move Well: the "Prices" column is about 180px tall beside a 520px portrait, leaving a big hole.
Fix: Stats: 3+2 on mobile or a single row that scrolls. Use grid-auto-flow: dense, or make the last item span. Give level-card titles min-height: 2lh. Let the Move Well prices span 2 columns beside a smaller portrait.

Congested design and spacing

12Medium

Hotels & Services is a wall of small text

/hotels-and-services

Four tight columns, each row holding a name on the left and a right-aligned 13px description or price. In "Eat", "Getting around" and "Work & wellness" both sides wrap to two lines, so the rows lose their rhythm. Twenty-plus rows per column, with nothing to scan by.

Fix: Use 2 columns at most. Use tabs (Stay / Eat / Getting around / Wellness) with one item per row: name, a one-line description underneath, then price and walking time as a tag. Add a map pin or link per place.
13Medium

The 43-course list is overwhelming

/continuing-education "Course list"

The list shows 43 titles in 3 columns. Many wrap to 2 lines, and each has an hours badge. It reads as a dense index, and the search and filters sit far above most of the list.

Fix: Group by topic (Spine, Lower body, Special populations, Repertoire), show 9–12 with "Show all 43", or make each group an accordion.
14Medium

Address and hours repeat back to back, and the footer is very long on mobile

Home, Contact, footer on every page

"Find us in Nyuh Kuning" (address, two hours tables, phone, directions) is followed immediately by the footer card with the same address, hours and directions. On mobile the footer is about 1.6 screens tall: tagline, phone, email, socials, address card, hours card and 4 link groups (21 links).

Fix: Keep the full location block on Contact only. Slim the footer to logo, one address line, phone and WhatsApp, and socials. On mobile, collapse the 4 link groups into accordions.
15Medium

The home intro statement looks disabled at rest

Home intro (ScrubText)

The brand's core promise ("We want to empower you…") starts at 16% opacity, about 1.3:1 contrast, and only darkens word by word as you scroll. On load it looks like greyed-out placeholder text, on mobile too.

Fix: Start at 40–50% opacity and finish the scrub earlier (end: "bottom 70%"), or show the text at full strength and animate a subtle underline instead.

/pricing-policies-list at 390px

The price table has min-width: 34rem inside a scroll container. Only "Single" is visible, and the 5-pack and 10-pack columns are clipped at the edge (a stray "5" peeks in). Nothing signals that the table scrolls. The tab bar above is also truncated ("Group Refo…") with no fade or arrow.

17Medium

On instructor pages, the name and booking button sit below the fold

All 6 instructor pages, mobile

The portrait takes 72svh, so the first screen shows only a photo. The name, role and "Book with…" button need almost two screens of scrolling.

Fix: Cap the portrait at about 55svh on mobile and overlay the name and role on a bottom scrim, or put the name above the photo.

UX, content and information architecture

18Critical

Workshop dates have passed

/move-well-series

The three workshops are listed for Thu 7, 14 and 21 May 2026. Today is 21 October 2026, so all three dates have passed, but they are still presented as upcoming and bookable. Only the 24 October full day is upcoming.

Fix: Store dates as ISO strings and hide past sessions (or mark them "Past" automatically). Show "Next dates announced soon, join the waitlist" when none remain.
19High

Too many booking paths, and some leave the site

Site-wide

The header "Book Now" opens WhatsApp. The hero "Book a Class" goes to the schedule page, and "Private Session" opens WhatsApp. CTA bands offer WhatsApp, Mindbody and Email, and the contact form sends via WhatsApp or Email. 22 links send visitors to the old WordPress site, including both registration forms, the course calendar, the client profile, all blog posts and 8 program pages. Visitors leave the new design at the moment they convert.

Fix: Define one primary action per intent: Group class → Mindbody, Private / Rolfing® / GYROTONIC® → WhatsApp, Training → registration form. Rebuild the registration forms and the course calendar in Next before retiring WordPress, and open any remaining legacy links in the same tab with a clear label.
20Medium

Pricing tabs mix people and services

/pricing-policies-list

The tabs are "Sook Fun · Ray · Pilates · Group Reformer · Rental & Extras". Two are instructors and three are services, so a visitor who wants "a private Pilates session" can't tell which tab applies. The 22-hour cancellation policy is also restated on Group Schedule ("Class policy"), Pricing and the Rental terms, in slightly different wording each time.

Fix: Make the tabs services (Private, Duet, Group Reformer, Rolfing®, Rental). Show instructor tiers as columns or a toggle inside. Keep the policies in one component reused everywhere.

23Medium

One display serif for everything, including 11–13px UI text

Marcellus is a single-weight (400) display face, and both --font-display and --font-sans point at it. It is used for form labels, schedule badges (11.2px), meta labels (13px uppercase with tracking), footer fine print and table figures. The nav fakes bold with -webkit-text-stroke. The result is low legibility at small sizes and no weight hierarchy.

Fix: Keep Marcellus for headings and add a humanist text face for body and UI (for example Mulish, Jost or Lato via next/font). Set a floor of 13px for labels and 16px for body, and use real weights instead of text-stroke.
24Low

Tap targets under 44px

Measured at 390px

  • Footer links: 39px tall (every page)
  • "Plan your first visit": 26px · "See Jumpstart": 26px · "Download the catalogue": 23px
  • CTA band links "Pricing & Policies" / "Group Class Schedule": 35px
  • Contact email links: 28px · Pricing tab "Ray": 32px wide
Fix: Add min-h-11 inline-flex items-center to inline text links, matching the existing Button.
25Low

Performance and hosting

26Medium

Every video depends on the old WordPress host

All 12 MP4s (up to 140 MB each, single bitrate) stream from movementmatters-bali.com/wp-content/uploads. When WordPress is retired, every video breaks, and mobile visitors download the full high-res file.

Fix: Move them to YouTube (unlisted), Mux or Cloudflare Stream for adaptive streaming. Don't put MP4s in public/ on Vercel.
27Low

4.4 MB catalogue PDF

public/assets/body-harmonics-catalogue.pdf is now the largest file in the deployment. Recompress it (for example with Ghostscript -dPDFSETTINGS=/ebook, typically 70–80% smaller) or link to Body Harmonics' hosted copy.

Storage optimisation applied

Every photo was stored in public/assets and imported through next/image, so each one shipped twice: once as a raw public file and once as a hashed copy in .next/static/media. Sources were also up to 3264px and 2.3 MB.

  • Moved all imported images to assets/images/ (outside public/), so each ships once. Only URL-linked files remain in public/assets: the PDFs, the favicon and a new 1200×630 og.jpg.
  • New scripts/optimize-images.mjs: caps landscape photos at 2048px wide and portraits at 1800px tall, re-encodes with mozjpeg at quality 80 (progressive) and compresses PNGs losslessly. It runs at the end of npm run assets.
  • next.config.ts: deviceSizes now stops at 2048 (no 3840px variants for Vercel to generate and store), and minimumCacheTTL is 31 days for the content-hashed images.
  • The fetch, trim and logo scripts now write to the new folders, so re-downloading won't refill public/. The README documents the rule.
  • Verified: clean production build passes, all 25 routes prerender, 0 broken images on Home, Media, Sook Fun Chen and Reformer Classes, and the PDFs, favicon and OG image resolve.
